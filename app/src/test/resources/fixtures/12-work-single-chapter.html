Better To Die Numb (Than To Feel It All)

Sokeyy

Summary:

Harry finds a dagger whilst helping to clean Grimmauld Place. He doesn't mean to do it. Not really. He just means to make a few short cuts, like he normally does. But suddenly the cuts are much deeper than normal, and the blood starts pouring and the next thing he knows, the world starts to go blurry. He just wanted to see his parents.

Written for Whumptober 2026 Day 18 - Suicide Attempt | Isolation Sickness

Notes:

Harry Potter makes a very good punching bag. Let's do it again!

Title taken from Growing Sideways by Noah Kahan

(See the end of the work for more notes.)

Work Text:

Blood is dripping down Sirius’ forearms, it’s staining his robes and the floorboards beneath his feet. The place looks like a murder scene with bloody towels strewn about the place, Mrs Weasley’s wand has been forgotten on the countertop along with the bloody dagger.

His stomach churns as the smell of blood burns in his nose and he can taste it in his mouth from where it had spurted in his face. A bloody hand lands gently on his shoulder and he looks behind him to see Remus, equally spattered with blood, looking as shell-shocked as he feels. The sound of Walburga Black’s screeching echoes up the stairs, and judging by the grunting and huffing coming from the landing as well, somebody must be trying to shut the curtains.

Remus squeezes Sirius’ shoulder and he tries to shake himself back to reality. Hermione and the Weasley children are sitting on Harry’s bed, where they had been banished to only minutes ago, staring wide eyed into the bathroom. Sirius forces himself to gather his thoughts and pull himself together.

‘Right,’ he says tersely. ‘Ron, Hermione, go wash yourselves off and put some clean clothes on. Kreacher will take what you’re wearing now and then we’ll go,’

‘Sirius, are you sure you should—’

‘I don’t care, Remus,’ Sirius cuts him off, trying to keep his voice level and free of the anger, or the fear that burns in his chest. ‘Get cleaned up and we’ll meet everyone at St Mungo’s,’

Remus only nods and ushers the children out of the room, guiding them to other bathrooms where they can clean the blood off of their hands. Sirius washes and re-dresses himself as quickly as he can, then leaves one simple order for Kreacher: clean up.

Something about the severity of the situation must break through to Kreacher, because he merely nods his head and assures Sirius that it will be done. Ron, Hermione and Remus meet him in the front hall, where they are speaking in hushed voices to Tonks and Moody, but they fall silent when Sirius descends the last of the stairs to stand with them.

Hermione turns to Sirius, her lower lip wobbling and eyes watery.

‘Do you think Harry will be okay?’ she whispers anxiously. Sirius tries to offer her a reassuring smile, but it likely comes out as more of a grimace.

‘Let’s go find out,’ he responds simply.

***

Harry should really be helping Ron, Hermione and the others finish cleaning the drawing room. But he can hear them laughing from two floors up and the thought of being in that room makes his stomach churn.

They expect him to be happy. Happy to be here, happy to be together, happy to be alive. Harry honestly can’t remember what happiness feels like. It’s as though something Voldemort did to him in that graveyard gave him amnesia and he has forgotten how to feel anything. His brain has put a block on it and all he feels is empty.

He doesn’t want to laugh. He doesn’t want to be happy or to smile or to listen to the twins’ jokes. He just wants to lie here on the bathroom floor forever. It’s been two hours since he threw his lunch up into the toilet, and he is still lying here. He can’t move.

The Order meeting has no doubt started, and then soon after Mrs Weasley will be forcing more food down him, but Harry doesn’t want to eat. He’s not hungry and eating only makes him feel sick. Everything he has eaten ends up coming back up within an hour, and Harry is tired of it.

Perhaps if he lies still enough, nobody will be able to see him. Perhaps they will ignore him and allow Harry to drift into nothing and nobody will ever bother him again. Perhaps then he will get to meet his parents.

God, it’s all he can think about lately. Seeing them in the graveyard was different to anything else. It was nothing like staring at them in a mirror, or committing photos of them to memory. They were real, they were right there with him and Harry hardly got the chance to look at their faces.

Harry wants so desperately to see them. To have them. All he’s ever wanted is to have someone. Growing up with the Dursleys was like growing up in a cave made of ice. He spent his days frozen to the bone, desperate for the ghost of a warmth he hardly knew. Even at Hogwarts, he is still alone. Nobody truly stands by him, even Ron abandoned him this year and if Ron can abandon him then anyone can. Even though he doesn’t know his parents, he’s sure that they would never abandon him again if they had the chance.

Perhaps Harry should give them the chance. Perhaps they deserve that much. Perhaps he deserves that much.

An ornate dagger hangs in Harry’s limp fingers, swaying to and fro where it balances in the crook of two knuckles. He had found it earlier in one of the rooms they were cleaning. It was in a dusty old cabinet next to the locket they found, and Harry had put it in his pocket without thinking. When he had been taken from Privet Drive, he had left his old razor blade under his mattress and under the watchful eyes of everybody here, Harry hasn’t been able to find and take anything sharp enough for days.

But he found this dagger, and he had glanced around quickly before nicking it and the relief that had flooded through him at the blade sitting snugly against his thigh in his pocket had been incredible. And now he is here, lying on the bathroom floor, staring at it.

He has a decision to make. He could do what he normally does and make three short, shallow cuts on his left arm and leave it there. Or he could go deeper. He could do something else. Try something new. Maybe even see his parents again.

Harry shouldn’t do it. Not here, and not like this. He can’t let it be Ron or Hermione who find him, or even Sirius. It would destroy them, and Harry has caused enough destruction in his life. He should have done it when he was still at the Dursleys. Let his aunt or uncle find him and let them deal with the consequences. It probably would have taken until the first of September for everybody else to notice he was missing. It’s too late now. Maybe he can find somewhere safe to do it at Hogwarts, somewhere that a teacher would find him. But not Snape. God, that might just be worse than somebody he cares about finding him.

Either way, now is not the time to do it.

Harry drags himself up off of the floor and stumbles over to the sink, extending his left arm over the porcelain bowl. He can see the scar left there by Wormtail’s dagger, the dark red line that slits down his arm and he wonders how Pettigrew managed to be so careful as to not cut him too deep.

He can hear Cedric’s last words ringing in his ear. Not the last words he spoke before he died, no, the last words he ever spoke to Harry. His desperation in begging Harry to take his body back to his father, to not let him rot there in that graveyard. Harry can see the blank look on Cedric’s face once death had stolen all emotion from it. He remembers Amos Diggory’s sobs, Professor McGonagall’s horrified gasp and prompt proclamation to the faculty that a boy had been murdered.

Murdered.

It’s an awful word.

It’s what happened to his parents.

It happens before Harry realises what he’s doing. All he knows is that he wants to see his parents. And suddenly he has taken the dagger to both of his forearms, slicing them from the crease of his elbow down to his wrist. Blood explodes from his wrists and then he hits the floor.

Fear grips his chest as he looks at the mess flooding the bathroom, the blood that was supposed to trickle into the sink is gushing onto the floor as his arms hang limply in his lap. His jeans are sticky and Harry’s breath comes in short gasps as he stares at the scene unfolding around him.

Oh fuck.

Fuck, Harry didn’t mean to do that. Fuck, fuck, fuck. What if Sirius finds him? Shit, he can’t let Sirius see him like this. Or Ron, or Hermione or any of them.

But it’s too late.

There’s a loud bang and the bathroom door flies open, revealing Fred standing with his siblings and Hermione behind him, wand raised. His wand clatters to the floor as his jaw drops open and suddenly Fred is on his knees next to Harry.

‘Towel! George, get towels!’ he shouts, grabbing Harry’s bath towel down from the rung behind him and jamming it against Harry’s arm. Harry doesn’t even feel the pain. He barely makes out Hermione screaming for Sirius and he tries to tell her to not let him come in, but his voice won’t work. He can feel everything slipping away until all he can see is flashes of red hair bobbing around in front of him.

Sirius is going to find him like this.

He’ll never be able to tell Sirius how sorry he is.

He didn’t mean it.

***

Snape is just finishing his report from the last meeting of the Death Eaters when Sirius hears it.

A blood-curdling scream echoes down the hallway, soon muffled by the sound of Sirius’ mother’s foul wailing. Chairs scrape as everybody stands in tandem, pulling wands out and rushing for the door. Just as Molly Weasley releases the Locking Charm on the door and throws it open, Hermione’s voice cuts through the house.

‘Sirius!’ she shrieks, loud enough to drown out the noise that Mrs Black is making.

Sirius’ heart jumps into his throat as he sprints up the stairs, a thunder of footsteps echoing behind him as every witch and wizard in the house runs up the stairs, following the sound of commotion.

It’s Harry. Sirius knows it’s Harry, or it would have been his Godson’s voice reverberating around the house, but it’s not. He can hear Hermione shouting, and the twins but Sirius cannot hear a word from Harry.

It feels like years pass by the time Sirius is skidding around the corner into Harry’s bedroom, but it can only have been moments. It takes a moment for Sirius to realise where everybody is, because Harry’s bedroom is empty, but he can hear the commotion.

And then he turns to the doorway to his bathroom.

Sirius has never seen anything like it.

There is blood everywhere. Ginny is standing in horror in the doorway, the only one who seems to be relatively blood-free. Fred and Ron are kneeling down, holding somebody’s arms in their hands, pressing towels down onto the wounds which spurt blood even through the fabric. George is pulling more towels out of every drawer in the room, and then Hermione comes barging through from behind Sirius carrying even more towels.

‘Sirius! Help!’ Hermione shouts as she passes, passing her towels on to Ron and tossing the red-soaked ones into the bathtub.

Her frenzied words snap Sirius out of his daze as he and Molly rush forward at the same moment. Remus grabs Ginny and pulls her out of the way. But it’s Snape who gets into the bathroom first.

‘Everybody out of the way!’ Snape orders, brandishing his wand and pushing Hermione and George back into the bedroom.

As everybody moves to make way, Sirius finally gets a glimpse at the pale face of the boy slumped against the wall. Sirius has known that it is Harry since he heard Hermione’s scream, but knowing it and seeing Harry inches from death, soaked in his own blood and staring blankly at the people around him are two entirely different things.

Snape kneels down in the pool of blood without a second thought, Molly close by his side, and Remus is on Sirius’ left, taking over from Ron and sending the boy away. Moody has already ushered Fred out of the way and is squeezing Harry’s arm so tight that his knuckles are white. Still, Sirius stands and stares.

‘It’s alright, Harry, we’ve got you,’ Remus is telling him firmly. ‘Just stay with us, Harry, stay awake,’

Harry groans and his head rolls over towards Remus. And Sirius is still standing and watching, terrified and completely useless. He should be the one reassuring Harry, telling him that everything is going to be okay.

Fuck, it has to be okay. Harry has to be okay. Sirius can’t live in a world where he isn’t.

Sirius ends up on his knees, sticky blood seeping through his robes and he drags himself over to Harry as Molly and Snape continue bustling around him, muttering incantations and cursing under their breath.

‘Come on, Harry,’ Sirius chokes out, grabbing Harry’s thigh and squeezing it tight. As Remus adjusts his hold on the towel he’s holding to Harry’s arm, blood spurts out into Sirius’ face, but he doesn’t flinch. ‘Just breathe, Harry, everything’s going to be okay,’

‘Nothing’s working!’ Molly cries helplessly.

‘Severus!’ Arthur Weasley calls from behind, appearing in the doorway with an armful of potions. Snape stands, leaving Molly to try and stem the bleeding that keeps spewing from Harry’s arms, hands shaking as she works.

Sirius looks around the room, trying to figure out how Harry has managed to do this to himself. What did Sirius miss? He saw the scars on Harry’s arms the first day he was here, and he’s tried to be so careful not to let anybody leave something sharp lying around.

And then he says it.

‘Fuck!’ he shouts as he lunges for the dagger lying in the bloody porcelain sink.

‘What is it?’ Remus asks hurriedly. ‘What did he use?’

‘The dagger! Shit! Fuck, it’s- it’s the—’

‘We need to get him to St. Mungo’s,’ Remus calls out. ‘Alastor, we need to get him to the kitchen fireplace, help me move him,’

‘Wait, he needs to take this first,’ Snape interrupts, kneeling back down and inadvertently pushing Sirius out of the way as he lifts a vial of potion to Harry’s mouth. He takes his other hand and squeezes Harry’s jaw open, tilting his head back.

Harry coughs and chokes as Snape pours the vial down his throat and Sirius has to fight with himself to not drag Snape away. He hates the man with a burning passion, but right now he might be the only person keeping Harry from slipping away.

‘Right, come on, time to go,’ Molly announces, climbing over Sirius and Remus to get behind Harry and with strength that Sirius didn’t know she possesses, she lifts Harry into her arms, moving carefully so that Moody and Remus can keep their grips tight on Harry’s arms.

Manoeuvring Harry out of the bathroom is difficult and it takes all of them to get him safely through the doorway. Snape is shouting, and Sirius has never heard him shout, at least not like this, and Molly is barking orders through tears and just before they carry through the doorway to the stairs, Remus stops.

‘Wait,’ he orders and everybody freezes. Remus’ eyes are locked on Sirius and he can’t see much through his blurred vision, but he can see the knowing look on his face. ‘Arthur, take Harry,’

‘Remus, we have to move—’

‘Now, Arthur!’ Remus roars and Arthur spurs to action, carefully taking over Remus’ grasp on Harry’s arm and then they’re on the move again.

Sirius turns back to look at the bathroom where blood drips down the walls and pools on the floor. Harry’s blood. It’s all Harry’s.

What would James and Lily say? How could Sirius let this happen? How could he be so stupid as to not notice that Harry took the dagger?

Sirius knew it was in the cabinet. He even meant to check and make sure that he was the one to take the cursed blade out. It’s supposed to cause irreversible damage. Basic healing spells don’t work; only time will. Sirius would know. He has more than a few badly healed scars from that dagger.

But none as bad as the ones Harry has inflicted on himself.

How could Sirius be so stupid? Once again, he has failed his best friends. Once again, he has failed a child he swore to protect. All Sirius does is fail.

And this might be his biggest failure yet.
